Why SEO Matters in the hospitality & restaurants Sector

Diners and guests search for somewhere to eat or stay right now, usually on a phone. Showing up in map results with good photos and reviews decides who gets the booking.

SEO Challenges for hospitality & restaurants Businesses

  • Map pack competition in busy areas
  • Dependence on booking platforms such as OpenTable, Booking.com and Tripadvisor
  • Seasonal and weekday demand swings
  • Menu, event and venue pages that are image-only and invisible to search engines
