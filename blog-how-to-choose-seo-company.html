How to Choose the Right SEO Company

Published October 7, 2026 · 7 min read

The right SEO partner can transform your lead flow. The wrong one can waste months and even damage your website. Use this guide to compare agencies with confidence.

Define Your Goals First

Before talking to agencies, decide what success means: more phone calls, form enquiries, online sales or entry into a new country. Clear goals make proposals easier to compare.

Questions to Ask Every SEO Company

  1. What will you do in the first 90 days? Expect an audit, keyword research and a prioritised plan.
  2. How do you build links? The answer should involve content, outreach and relevance, never bulk purchases.
  3. How do you report results? Look for tracking of leads and revenue, not only rankings.
  4. Who will work on my account? You should know your account manager and how to reach them.
  5. Do you understand my target country? UK, US and Indian markets each need different research, language and local signals.
  6. What are the contract terms? Prefer short notice periods and clear deliverables.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • Guaranteed first-page or number-one rankings
  • Very low prices with vague deliverables
  • Secret methods or refusal to explain the work
  • Ownership of your accounts or website held by the agency
  • Large numbers of cheap directory or spam links

What Good SEO Reporting Looks Like

A good monthly report shows organic traffic, keyword movement, leads from organic search, completed work and planned next steps. It should be written in plain language so you can understand what is happening and why.

Compare Proposals Fairly

Compare scope, not just price. Check what is included in content, technical work, link building and reporting, and ask how each item supports your goals.
