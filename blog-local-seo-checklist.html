Local SEO Checklist for Small Businesses

Published October 21, 2026 · 8 min read

If customers find you by searching “near me” or a city name, local SEO is the fastest route to more calls and visits. Work through this checklist to strengthen every signal Google uses for local rankings.

Google Business Profile

  1. Claim and verify your profile. Make sure you are the owner and the listing is verified.
  2. Choose the right primary category. It is one of the strongest local ranking factors. Add relevant secondary categories.
  3. Complete every field. Add hours, services, products, attributes, website link and a clear business description.
  4. Upload real photos regularly. Show your location, team, work and products.
  5. Post updates. Share offers, news and projects at least weekly.

Reviews and Reputation

  1. Ask happy customers for reviews. Share a direct review link by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  2. Reply to every review. Be polite and professional, especially with negative feedback.

Citations and Consistency

  1. Keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Inconsistent details confuse search engines.
  2. List your business in relevant directories. Focus on trusted, country-appropriate sites and industry-specific listings.

Your Website

  1. Create a page for each service and location. Write unique content mentioning real local details, not copied templates.
  2. Add local schema markup and an embedded map. Include your address, phone and opening hours.
  3. Make the site fast and mobile-friendly. Most local searches happen on phones. Add click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons.

Measuring Progress

Track map rankings, profile views, calls, direction requests and website enquiries each month. Compare them with the previous month to see what is working.
