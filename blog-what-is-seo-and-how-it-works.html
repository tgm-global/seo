What Is SEO and How Does It Work?

Published October 28, 2026 · 9 min read

Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the practice of improving your website so it appears higher in unpaid search results. Because most online journeys begin with a search, ranking well means more visitors, more enquiries and more sales without paying for each click.

How Search Engines Work

Search engines like Google follow three main steps:

  1. Crawling: automated bots follow links to discover pages.
  2. Indexing: the engine analyses each page and stores it in a huge database.
  3. Ranking: when someone searches, the engine selects the most relevant, helpful and trustworthy pages and orders them.

Your job is to make it easy for search engines to find your pages and clear that they deserve to rank.

The Four Pillars of SEO

1. Keyword research and search intent

Keywords are the phrases people type into search engines. Good research finds terms with real demand and buying intent. Equally important is intent: someone searching “what is a CRM” wants information, while “best CRM for small business” is comparing options, and “buy CRM software” is ready to act. Match each page to one clear intent.

2. On-page SEO and content

On-page SEO covers the elements on your pages: title tags, meta descriptions, headings, body content, images and internal links. Content should answer the searcher’s question fully, be easy to read and show real expertise.

3. Technical SEO

Technical SEO ensures search engines can access your site. It includes fast loading speed, mobile-friendly design, secure HTTPS, clean URL structure, XML sitemaps and structured data. Learn more in our technical SEO audit guide.

4. Off-page SEO and authority

Backlinks from trusted websites act like recommendations. For local businesses, consistent business listings and reviews also build trust. See our link building services and local SEO checklist.

How Long Does SEO Take?

SEO is a long-term investment. Many sites see early movement within 3 to 4 months, with stronger results from months 6 to 12. Speed depends on competition, your site’s history and how consistently you publish and improve content.

Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

  • Targeting keywords with no buying intent
  • Publishing thin or copied content
  • Ignoring mobile speed
  • Buying low-quality backlinks
  • Not tracking leads, only rankings

Getting Started with SEO

Start by auditing your current site, choosing a handful of high-value keywords, fixing technical issues and creating helpful content for each service you offer. If you prefer expert support, our team can review your website for free.

Good SEO is not about tricking search engines. It is about being the most helpful result for the person searching.
