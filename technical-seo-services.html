Technical SEO Services That Remove Ranking Roadblocks

Great content cannot rank if search engines struggle to crawl, render or index your website. We fix the technical foundation first.

What Is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO covers everything that affects how search engines access and understand your website: site architecture, page speed, mobile usability, indexing, redirects, structured data and security.

Our Technical SEO Checklist

  • Full crawl audit to find broken links, redirect chains and orphan pages
  • Core Web Vitals improvements: LCP, INP and CLS
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration
  • Index coverage review in Google Search Console
  • Canonical, hreflang and pagination fixes for multi-country sites
  • Structured data for organisation, products, articles and FAQs
  • Mobile usability and HTTPS security checks
  • JavaScript rendering and lazy-loading review

International and Multi-Country SEO

If you target the UK, USA and India, correct hreflang tags and URL structure prevent the wrong version appearing in the wrong country. We plan and implement this cleanly so each market sees the right page.

What You Receive

You get a prioritised issue list ranked by impact and effort, developer-ready instructions, and monthly monitoring so new problems are caught early.

Technical SEO FAQs

Will technical SEO improve my rankings on its own?

It removes barriers and often brings quick gains, but the best results come when it is combined with strong content and authority building.

Do you work with our developers?

Yes. We provide clear tickets and can work with your in-house team or agency, or implement changes directly on supported platforms.

Which platforms do you support?

WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Webflow, custom builds and modern JavaScript frameworks.
