Why SEO Matters in the construction & home improvement Sector

Home projects and repairs are often urgent. People search on their phones, compare a few local companies and call the one that looks most trustworthy.

SEO Challenges for construction & home improvement Businesses

  • Emergency searches that need fast mobile pages and click-to-call
  • Separate pages needed for every service and every town served
  • Seasonal demand
  • Proof of quality: project galleries, reviews and accreditations

Compliance note: Display trade accreditations, insurance and licences, such as Gas Safe or NICEIC in the UK, and state or local contractor licences in the USA.
